Brand summary

CINEVO is a private media home for the libraries people already own: Plex, Jellyfin and folders on their computer. It runs on consent. Nothing appears until the user chooses it, sharing is by username and never by files, and there are no ads or subscriptions. The identity is a faceted play triangle in a vivid spectrum: a private cinema that is bright, modern and playful rather than solemn.

Built from your supplied logo, app icon and palette reference, plus copy from cinevo-omega-rho.vercel.app. Typeface is my read of the artwork (it looks like Poppins Bold); please confirm.
